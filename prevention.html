Cyber Safety & Prevention

🔑 Strong Passwords

Use unique and strong passwords for different accounts.

🔢 Never Share OTP

Never share OTP, PIN or passwords with anyone.

🔗 Check Links

Don't click suspicious links received through messages or email.

📱 Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication whenever available.

💳 Verify Payments

Always verify the receiver before making a payment.

🔄 Update Software

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

Stay Alert. Stay Safe.
