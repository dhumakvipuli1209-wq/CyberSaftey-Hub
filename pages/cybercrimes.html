Types of Cybercrime

Learn about common cyber threats.

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites are used to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

💳 Online Payment Fraud

Fraud involving UPI, cards, banking or other online payment methods.

🔐 Identity Theft

Using another person's personal information without permission.

📱 Social Media Fraud

Fake profiles, suspicious messages and social engineering attacks.

🛒 Online Shopping Fraud

Fake websites, fake products or payment scams.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software designed to damage systems or steal information.
