🎣 Can You Spot the Scam?

Learn how common online scams try to trick you.

🚨 Common Scam Examples

🎁 Prize Scam

Message: "Congratulations! You have won ₹50,000. Click this link to claim your prize."

⚠️ Warning Signs: Unexpected prize, urgency and a suspicious link.
🛡️ What should you do? Do not click the link. Verify the offer through the official source.

🏦 Fake KYC Scam

Message: "Your bank account KYC has expired. Update it immediately or your account will be blocked."

⚠️ Warning Signs: Threatening language, urgency and an unknown link.
🛡️ What should you do? Do not click the link or share your OTP. Contact the bank using its official website or app.

💸 UPI Payment Scam

Situation: Someone says they will send you money and asks you to enter your UPI PIN to receive it.

⚠️ Warning Sign: A UPI PIN is used to authorise payments, not to receive money.
🛡️ What should you do? Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.

📱 Fake Job Scam

Message: "Congratulations! You are selected for a work-from-home job. Pay ₹999 as a registration fee."

⚠️ Warning Signs: Asking for money before giving a job and unrealistic promises.
🛡️ What should you do? Verify the company and job offer before sharing personal information or making payments.

🤖 AI Voice Scam

Situation: You receive a call that sounds like a family member asking for urgent money.

⚠️ Warning Sign: The caller creates panic and demands immediate payment.
🛡️ What should you do? Stay calm and verify the person's identity using another trusted method.

📦 Fake Delivery Scam

Message: "Your parcel could not be delivered. Pay ₹25 using this link to reschedule delivery."

⚠️ Warning Signs: Unknown delivery message, payment request and suspicious link.
🛡️ What should you do? Check your order through the official shopping or delivery app.

🧠 Stop. Check. Verify.

Don't let urgency or fear make your decision for you. Take a moment to verify before you click, share or pay.
