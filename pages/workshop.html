Cyber Awareness & Prevention Workshop

Build safer habits for everyday digital life.

1. Pause before you click

Check the sender, spelling, web address and unexpected urgency before opening a link or attachment.

2. Protect your accounts

Use unique passwords, enable multi-factor authentication and never share an OTP, PIN or password.

3. Make payments safely

Verify the recipient before paying. A UPI PIN authorises a payment; you do not enter it to receive money.

4. Verify urgent requests

If a friend or family member urgently asks for money, contact them through another trusted channel first.

5. Report suspected cybercrime

In India, call 1930 promptly for cyber financial fraud and report at the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in.

6. Practise what you learned

Visit the Scam Awareness page, then test your knowledge with the Cyber Detective Game and Quiz.
